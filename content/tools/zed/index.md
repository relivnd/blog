I'm a huge fan of Zed. At first, it was the speed that amazed me. Written in Rust, Zed feels dramatically faster to me than bulkier editors such as VS Code or full-featured IDEs such as IntelliJ IDEA. I've worked with both and think they're great in their own way, but my personal choice is Zed.

But Zed isn't just blazingly fast. It also offers features that make it stand out. Its [remote development](/posts/develop-remotely-with-zed-aws) feature makes it easy to connect to development servers, such as a simple EC2 instance, and its AI capabilities feel just right. For example, I can request inline changes or work with an agent without leaving my editing workflow. The LLM integrations support my work where and how I expect them to.
