chezmoi is my tool of choice for managing all my dotfiles across my machines. The small Go tool allows me to back up and keep all my config files in sync. Examples of config files I share across my machines are `.zshrc`, all my [Kiro CLI](/tools/kiro) & Claude Code configs or my [Zed](/tools/zed) config & keymaps. It's small, but capable. I even keep a copy of my [Brewfile](/tools/homebrew) in my config repository managed by chezmoi.
