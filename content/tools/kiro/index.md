My AI agent of choice is called Kiro. More precisely the Kiro CLI. While the Kiro IDE does me good service when creating demos or PoCs, I prefer using Kiro in combination with Zed. Thanks to its [Agent Client Protocol](https://kiro.dev/docs/cli/acp/) implementation, integrating it with Zed is a breeze.

You might ask: why are there two Kiro products? That's simple. In 2023, AWS acquired a startup called [fig](https://web.archive.org/web/20230605000718/https://fig.io/) and rebranded the product to Amazon Q for Developers CLI. With the announcement of the Kiro IDE, the Amazon Q for Developers CLI was renamed to Kiro CLI. Their different paths also explain the differences in features. The Kiro CLI is — like Zed — written in Rust and highly performant.
