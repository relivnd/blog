My AI agent of choice is called Kiro. More precisely, the Kiro CLI. While the Kiro IDE serves me well when creating demos or PoCs, I prefer using Kiro in combination with [Zed](/tools/zed). Thanks to its [Agent Client Protocol](https://kiro.dev/docs/cli/acp/) implementation, integrating it with Zed is a breeze.

You might ask: why are there two Kiro products? That's simple. In 2023, AWS acquired a startup called [Fig](https://web.archive.org/web/20230605000718/https://fig.io/) and rebranded the product as Amazon Q Developer CLI. With the announcement of the Kiro IDE, the Amazon Q Developer CLI was renamed to Kiro CLI. Their different paths also explain the differences in features.
